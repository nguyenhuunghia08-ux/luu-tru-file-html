Sarah's Healthy Daily Life

Sarah lives in a small, beautiful town in Italy. She is twenty-four years old and she is a primary school teacher. Sarah loves her job because she likes working with children.

Every day, Sarah gets up at six o'clock in the morning. She starts her day with a glass of warm water. Then, she goes to the park near her house. She runs for thirty minutes. She thinks sport is very important for a healthy life. After her run, she has a shower and eats a healthy breakfast. She usually eats eggs, fresh fruit, and drinks a cup of green tea.

At eight o'clock, she walks to school. The school is not far from her house. She teaches English and Art. Her students are seven years old. They are very friendly and happy.

In the afternoon, Sarah finishes work at three o'clock. She goes to the local market. She buys fresh vegetables, fish, and pasta for dinner. She likes cooking healthy meals at home. Sometimes, her friend Maria comes to her house for dinner. They eat together and talk about their day.

In the evening, Sarah reads a book or listens to quiet music. She does not watch TV because she prefers to relax. She goes to bed early, at ten o'clock. She feels happy and ready for a new day.
